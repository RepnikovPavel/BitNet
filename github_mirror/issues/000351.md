### Comment by @kyang-06 at 2026-02-02T07:57:32Z

ran into the same issue. I use A6000 GPU.
The solution is modify `compile.sh` to meet your gpu device architecture. For me, it is:
`nvcc -std=c++17 -Xcudafe --diag_suppress=177 --compiler-options -fPIC -lineinfo --shared bitnet_kernels.cu -lcuda -gencode=arch=compute_86,code=sm_86 -o libbitnet.so`

Note that `code=compute_86` will still produce wrong answer, must be `sm_86`
