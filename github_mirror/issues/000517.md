### Comment by @vitorgamer58 at 2026-03-30T02:53:20Z

> In summary, the problem isn't with rope.freq_base, but rather with `batch_size` and `ubatch_size`.

Findings:
- -b (logical batch) has no effect on stability, works up to 2048
- -ub (physical ubatch) is the actual constraint, max stable = 16
- rope_freq_base is read correctly from GGUF metadata automatically

@Eddie-Wang1120 Would it be necessary to change anything in the quantization to support a larger ubatch-size?
